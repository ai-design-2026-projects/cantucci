\section{Ethical Considerations}\label{sec:ethics}

\paragraph{Synthetic Oracle Limitations}
Because all evaluations rely on an LLM-simulated oracle rather than human subjects, our findings reflect agent-to-agent dynamics rather than authentic human behavior or preferences.

\paragraph{Model and Embedding Bias}
The language and embedding models driving the system are trained predominantly on Western, English-language data, inheriting its cultural biases. 

\paragraph{LLM-as-a-Judge Risks}
Employing an LLM to evaluate another LLM introduces severe structural risks regarding evaluation accuracy, blind spots, and hallucinations.
