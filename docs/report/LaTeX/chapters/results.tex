\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Experimental Setup}

\paragraph{Dataset:} The evaluation ran against a compact catalogue, held out from the production dataset, of \textbf{288 films}.

\paragraph{Conditions:} Two conditions were compared.
The \textbf{conversational} condition represents our full multi-agent system.
The \textbf{baseline} is a single-prompt alternative: a single model call per turn receives the raw film list, the current cluster state, and the dialogue transcript, then emits the entire updated grouping, the chosen operation, and the conversational response in a single output.
This baseline completely bypasses embeddings, density-based clustering, concept axes, clarifying loops, and dedicated labeling agents.

\paragraph{Models:} The system, oracle, and judge run on three different model families (Table~\ref{tab:models}).

\paragraph{Procedure:} Each persona/seed pair initialized a session driven by the simulated oracle until termination or budget exhaustion, after which all metric and judge dimensions were logged.
This process yielded \textbf{35 conversational} sessions and \textbf{30 baseline} sessions.
All reported metrics are extracted directly from the resulting database and computed using the confidence intervals from Equation~\ref{eq:ci}.

\subsection{Qualitative Results}

Two example sessions are shown below: one the oracle rated a success, and one it abandoned. The
exchanges are condensed summaries that capture the core of each conversation, not verbatim
transcripts.

\subsubsection{\textbf{Success}: \texttt{worldcinema}}
\textbf{\\Persona:} verbose, patience~$0.37$.
\\
\textbf{Intent description:} ``Break the catalogue down by language to see what non-English
traditions are represented, then zoom into the French-language films and see how they spread between
intimate personal stories and broader societal canvases.''
\\
\begin{quote}\small
\textbf{Oracle:} Split the entire collection by its original language.\\
\textbf{System:} Partitioned 288 movies by original language into 13 groups.\\
\textbf{Oracle:} Zoom in on the French-language cluster.\\
\textbf{System:} Focused on French-language films: 7 movies retained, all others discarded.\\
\textbf{Oracle:} Undo, then split the \emph{entire} catalogue by language again.\\
\textbf{System:} Partitioned 7 movies by original language into 1 group.\\
\textbf{Oracle:} We've run into a loop, so reset everything and re-cluster.\\
\textbf{System:} Session reset. Partitioned 288 movies into 13 groups.\\
\textbf{Oracle:} Split the French films by intimate-personal vs societal narrative, into 3 groups.\\
\textbf{System:} Split into 2 sub-clusters: \textsf{Intimate French}, \textsf{Societal French}.
\end{quote}

\subsubsection{\textbf{Failure}: \texttt{satire}}

\textbf{\\Persona:} terse, patience~$0.76$.
\\
\textbf{Intent description:} ``Keep only comedy films, then spread them along a spectrum
from warm-hearted romantic levity to biting satirical bite.''
\\
\begin{quote}\small
\textbf{Oracle:} keep only comedy films, then group them.\\
\textbf{System:} Filter matched 74 movies. Split into 2: \textsf{Asian Romantic Comedies Family
  Fantasy Comedies}, \textsf{Family Animated Adventures Family Fantasy Comedies}.\\
\textbf{Oracle:} split the \textsf{Asian Romantic\,\ldots} group into 3 groups.\\
\textbf{System:} Split into 4 sub-clusters.\\
\textbf{Oracle:} why is \emph{Don't Look Up} in the \textsf{Family Animated\,\ldots} group?\\
\textbf{System:} ``Playdate'' is placed in this cluster due to\ldots\\
\textbf{Oracle:} focus on the Asian-romantic group, then split it by tone into 3.\\
\textbf{System:} Split into 3 sub-clusters: \textsf{Warm Romantic Comedies Asian Romantic Comedies
  Family Fantasy Comedies}, \textsf{Satirical \ldots}, \textsf{Light-hearted Romantic Comedies Asian
  Romantic Comedies Family Fantasy Comedies}.\\
\textbf{Oracle:} why is \emph{Mean Girls} in the \textsf{Warm Romantic Comedies\,\ldots} group?\\
\textbf{System:} ``Friends with Benefits'' fits this cluster because\ldots\\
\textbf{Oracle:} we are not making progress on the original intent.
\end{quote}
